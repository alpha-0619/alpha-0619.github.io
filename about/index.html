關於 Menu Check

Menu Check 把餐廳自己公布或傳給我們的菜單打成文字，附出處和查證日期，讓人出門前查得到最新的價錢。

資料從哪裡來

  • 只用餐廳官網、餐廳自己在粉專貼的菜單或公告，或餐廳傳給我們的菜單。
  • 部落格、外送平台、評論照片只拿來比對，不當資料來源。
  • 每一頁都寫出處和查證日期；價錢以店內為準。
  • 只放文字，不放店家照片。
  • 外文頁的菜名用店家自己的翻譯。

跟餐廳的關係

Menu Check 是獨立網站，不是任何一家餐廳的官方網站，跟這些餐廳沒有合作關係。

About (English)

Menu Check types out menus that restaurants publish themselves or send us, with the source and the date we checked, so you can see current prices before you go.

  • We use only the restaurant's own website, the menus or notices it posts on its own pages, or the menu it sends us.
  • Blogs, delivery apps and review photos are used only for comparison, never as a source.
  • Every page says where the menu came from and when we checked it; the price at the restaurant is final.
  • Text only, no photos of the restaurant.
  • On pages in other languages, dish names are the restaurant's own translations.

Menu Check is an independent site. It is not the official site of any restaurant and has no connection with them.
