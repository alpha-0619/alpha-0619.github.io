我們做什麼

Menu Check 只用餐廳自己的菜單：店家官網、店家在粉專公布的價目，或店家傳給我們的菜單。我們把它打成文字，每一頁都寫出處和查證日期，讓人出門前就查得到現在的價錢。部落格、外送平台和評論照片只拿來比對，不當資料來源。價錢以店內為準。

台灣

台灣目前有 14 家。12 家餐廳用的是店家傳給我們、或店家自己在粉專公布的菜單，我們把菜單上的字抄成文字。可不可熟成茶行和路易莎咖啡兩家連鎖店，照官網的價目整理，每天自動重查一次。

永康牛肉麵館、兩喜號、周家蒸餃小籠包、阿村牛肉湯第二代、萬華源芳刈包這 5 家，店家自己有英文、日文或韓文菜單，我們另外做了外文頁。外國旅客在外文頁上按價錢點菜，再按一下，畫面就變成大字的中文點餐單和總價，可以直接拿給店員看。

曼谷

曼谷目前有 47 家餐廳的頁面：45 家取自餐廳自己公布的菜單，2 家是在店裡拍下的菜單，其中 38 家有價錢。

曼谷很多餐廳的菜單價格不含服務費和稅。有 16 家的菜單印明要另加 10% 服務費和 7% 稅；泰國稅務局規定服務費也要課稅，所以帳單大約是菜單價的 1.177 倍。這 16 家的頁面在每個價錢下面，算好帳單上大約多少。

What we do

Menu Check uses only the restaurant's own menu: its website, the prices it posts on its own social page, or the menu it sends us. We type it out as text, and every page says where the menu came from and when we checked it, so you can see current prices before you go. Blogs, delivery apps and review photos are used only for comparison, never as a source. The price at the restaurant is final.

Taiwan

Taiwan has 14 places so far. For 12 restaurants we use the menu the shop sent us or posted on its own page, typed out from the printed menu. The prices of two chains, Kebuke and Louisa Coffee, follow their official websites and are re-checked automatically every day.

Five shops have their own English, Japanese or Korean menus, and each of them has a page in that language. Tap the prices of what you want, then tap once more: the screen shows your order in large Chinese with the total, ready to show the staff.

Bangkok

Bangkok has pages for 47 restaurants: 45 from menus the restaurants publish themselves and 2 from menus photographed in the restaurant; 38 of them have prices.

Many Bangkok menus print prices before service charge and tax. Sixteen of our menus state a 10% service charge and 7% VAT. Thailand's Revenue Department counts service fees as part of the value that VAT is charged on, so the bill is about 1.177 times the menu price, and those pages show that amount under every price.
